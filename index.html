P = E ÷ tPower = energy ÷ time. So energy = power × time. Use watts and hours to get Wh.
P = I × VPower = current × voltage. So current = power ÷ voltage.
P = I² × RPower wasted as heat = current × current × resistance.

    Welcome, rover engineer

    You look after the power on a Mars rover. Each Mars day you plan the rover's jobs and work out four numbers:

    • the current the jobs need (in amps, A)
    • the power wasted as heat in the dusty wires (in watts, W)
    • the total energy the day uses (in watt-hours, Wh)
    • the cost of that energy (in credits)

    Two things can go wrong. If the current is bigger than the fuse limit, the fuse blows. If the energy is bigger than the battery capacity, the battery runs flat.

    Use a calculator and paper. Answers within 2% count as correct. Get something wrong and you lose a life. Lose three and the mission ends.
